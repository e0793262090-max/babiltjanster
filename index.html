About us (English)

Babylon tjänster AB (Swedish company reg. no. 559528-9280, VAT SE559528928001) is a private service bureau in Solna, Stockholm, Sweden. We help Iraqi customers in Sweden prepare documents and fill in forms for visas, certified translations, legalisations and civil-status records.

We are an independent private company. We are not an embassy, a consulate or a government authority, and we do not sell appointments — booking an appointment is free and customers make it themselves.
